RESEARCHLENS - CHAT EDITION

1. Extract the complete ZIP into a fresh folder.
2. Double-click START_WINDOWS.bat (Python 3.12 required).
3. Create an account and sign in.
4. Open AI connection in the sidebar.
5. Choose Groq - free tier, paste your Groq API key, click Check & connect Groq.
6. Attach your PDF from the sidebar and click Add files.
7. Choose a document in Answer using. Ask a question in Chat. Answers are written in English, with source references.
8. Export PDF when you want to save your research notes.

No manual .env editing is required. The in-app key is session-only and clears
on sign-out. Groq Free has request/token limits. Never share your API key.
If AI is not connected, Chat is disabled instead of returning copied passages.
Free passage search is separately available under Documents.

Example questions:
What is this document about?
Summarize this paper.
What methodology does it use?
What limitations are described?
Explain page 2. (Select one PDF in Answer using first.)
Compare pages 2 and 4.

The tutorial document is not a research paper. AI answers require internet.
